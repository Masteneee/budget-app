            Budget style

            The 50/30/20 rule splits your income into three parts: about 50% for needs (food, transport, school, bills), 30% for wants (fun and other), and 20% for savings. It's a starting point, not a strict law, so adjust it to fit your life.

            Money left in a category's budget is added to next month's budget for that category. Going over never reduces next month.

            When set, each expense also shows how many hours of work it cost. Leave empty to turn it off.
